Xbox Through the Years

Each Xbox generation brought new hardware, new services, and new expectations for console gaming.

Console Timeline

2001: Original Xbox

Microsoft entered the console market with strong hardware, a built in hard drive, and Halo.

2005: Xbox 360

Xbox Live, achievements, party chat, and multiplayer helped define the Xbox 360 generation.

2013: Xbox One

The Xbox One focused on entertainment at launch, then shifted toward games, Game Pass, and backward compatibility.

2020: Xbox Series X

The Xbox Series X focused on faster loading, stronger performance, 4K gaming, and backward compatibility.
