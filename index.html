    Overlapping Feedback

    • Light mode still needs more polish

    • Small movements or hover effects would make the site feel more alive
    • The design is clean, but some sections could use more personality

    Listed above are key points from the feedback I recieved from my grandpa, friend, and girlfriend on the first draft of my project. Decided to use them as it cast a broad net of age, demographic, and perspective. I can definitely see the points about lightmode needing to be polished more, mainly threw that in to score the bonus points. I also agree that it would be best to flesh out the pages more, and add some more stylish effects to make the site feel more alive. Overall I decided to focus on the overlapping points to improve the site.

    For the final revision I focused on improving the light mode, adding more personality to the pages. I also fluffed up the entire site with generated text to make it feel and look like a real site compairing the generations of xbox. I also added a comparison table for each iteration of xbox for each console generation. One point my grandfather made that I decided not to change was the contact page, as a gamer and someone who visits a lot of these sites the contact pages are always simple, just put your information and the complaint/feedback and send it. What I did well was pretty clear, the site was easy to understand and navigate. It also kept with the xbox theme well. Some things I wish I had considered early would be the setup for the page. Because I went back to add the comparison table i had to figure out where it would fit best. For the final revision, I had a lot of touching up to do on the lightmode, the amount of troubleshooting I did infuriated me, the amount of time i spent trying to figure out why every fix caused another issue somewhere else on the site was honestly one of the most frustrating parts of the project. Originally, only the background changed, which made a lot of the text, buttons, tables, and content boxes difficult to read. I spent a large amount of time researching different ways to make the light mode fully functional and visually consistent across the entire website. Most of which came up with with the fact i shouldve relied on the browsers built in system, but i thought and still believe that based on the rubric we needed a seperate button to control the toggles between the two modes.
